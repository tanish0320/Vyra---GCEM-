# Frontend asset notices

The eye incorporates **HRA Eye, Female, Right v1.3**, by Kristen Browne and Heidi
Schlehlein, https://doi.org/10.48539/HBM632.KTDP.379, under **CC BY4.0**
(https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). VYRA normalized, optimized, UV
mapped, textured and adapted the asset with sectional variants; added structures,
materials and presentation geometry are recorded in `public/anatomy/manifest.json`.
The homepage assembly film and posters render the adapted educational asset.

The layered fingertip is original VYRA artwork. Its generated surface textures
are appearance assets, not patient images. In-app source credits are preserved.

The two screening photographs under `tests/fixtures` are the team's supplied
permitted test samples; browser fixtures are confined to tests. Public anatomy
assets must never be submitted as real patient samples.

Source dependency licenses remain available in their packages/lockfile. The
existing shadcn/ui primitives are MIT licensed. Fonts are bundled through their
Fontsource packages; retain their license files when redistributing dependencies.
