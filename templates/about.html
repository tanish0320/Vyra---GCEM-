How the AI works

AnemiaVision AI uses a pretrained EfficientNet-B3 convolutional network to analyze smartphone images of the eye conjunctiva and fingernail bed. The model looks for color and texture patterns linked to reduced hemoglobin appearance, then produces a screening result with confidence and Grad-CAM explainability.
