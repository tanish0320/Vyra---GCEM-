Clinical Validation Integrity

The Patient Leakage Problem & Our Solution

In published smartphone healthcare AI, a widespread flaw is patient cross-contamination: images of the same patient (e.g. left eye vs. right eye, or burst photo shots under identical lighting) are split randomly between the training and testing sets.

When models memorize the room lighting or individual skin tone, they report inflated 95%+ "accuracy" that completely collapses in real-world clinic trials.

VYRA's Zero-Leakage Architecture:

We implemented a 2-tier deduplication and clustering pipeline prior to splitting:

  • Tier 1 — Exact SHA-256 Hashing: Identified and purged 2,375 identical image duplicates across cohorts.
  • Tier 2 — Perceptual 64-bit dHash: Clustered near-identical burst shots and angle variations using Hamming distance thresholds to consolidate patient identities without patient ID labels.

Feature Engineering

The 27-Dimensional Chromatic Feature Vector

In addition to the deep convolutional feature map from EfficientNet-B3, VYRA's dual-head predictor extracts a calibrated 27-dimensional chromatic tabular vector from each normalized region:

Color Ratios (6) Normalized RGB Channels

Channel proportions: R/(R+G+B), G/(R+G+B), B/(R+G+B), R/G, R/B, G/B to decouple luminance intensity from spectral reflectance.

CIE-LAB Erythema (6) a* & b* Chromaticity

Mean, median, and variance of a* (green-to-red axis, proxy for blood perfusion) and b* (blue-to-yellow axis, proxy for bilirubin/jaundice).

HSV Saturation (6) Vascular Saturation & Value

Hue angle, saturation richness, and value distribution to measure the blanching effect of depleted hemoglobin concentration.

Micro-Texture (9) Spatial Entropy & Contrast

Local Shannon entropy, Haralick contrast, and capillary edge gradient density to distinguish vessel branching from diffuse skin pallor.

Clinical Safety Rules

Operational Clinical Guardrails

The Fingernail Rule

Continuous Hemoglobin regression (g/dL) is strictly locked to the conjunctiva modality. The fingernail modality is programmatically restricted to binary screening classification, preventing clinically unsubstantiated continuous Hb claims without paired lab CBCs.

Automated ROI Gating

Automated YuNet face & ocular landmark localization isolates the palpebral conjunctiva microvascular zone, discarding uninformative iris, sclera, and facial skin.

Optical Quality Rejection Gate

Images with flash glare saturation (>15% specular reflection), extreme blur, or out-of-frame anatomy are rejected immediately with guidance to retake, avoiding false predictions.

Medical Disclaimer Mandate

VYRA is an AI-assisted screening prototype intended for Community Health Worker (CHW) triage. It never replaces laboratory venous CBC testing.
